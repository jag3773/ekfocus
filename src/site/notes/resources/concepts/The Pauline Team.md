# The Pauline Team
In Acts and the Epistles we see 68 people listed as part of Paul's extended missionary team. Various terms are used for these people, some of which may indicate particular roles. For example, "coworker", "fellow soldier", "benefactor", "deacon", "apostle", "elder", etc.

See [[resources/notes/2021-02-16 Paul's relational love over apostolic command\|2021-02-16 Paul's relational love over apostolic command]]

## Team Members
*68 people*
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Achaicus\|Achaicus]] - Corinthian delegate who visited Paul in Ephesus with [[resources/people/pauline team/Stephanas\|Stephanas]] and [[resources/people/pauline team/Fortunatus\|Fortunatus]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Alexander\|Alexander]] - Coppersmith in Ephesus who opposed Paul's message and caused him harm.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Ananias\|Ananias]] - Damascus disciple who laid hands on Paul after his conversion, restoring his sight.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Andronicus\|Andronicus]] - Jewish believer in Rome, fellow prisoner of Paul, outstanding among the apostles, in Christ before Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Apelles\|Apelles]] - Roman believer commended by Paul as "approved in Christ" after enduring a test of faith.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Apollos\|Apollos]] - Alexandrian Jewish teacher, eloquent and mighty in Scripture, instructed by [[resources/people/pauline team/Priscilla\|Priscilla]] and [[resources/people/pauline team/Aquila\|Aquila]] in Ephesus.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Apphia\|Apphia]] - Wife of [[resources/people/pauline team/Philemon\|Philemon]], co-addressee of Paul's letter, host of the Colossian house church.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Aquila\|Aquila]] - Jewish tentmaker from Pontus, husband of [[resources/people/pauline team/Priscilla\|Priscilla]], hosted house churches in Corinth, Ephesus, and Rome.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Archippus\|Archippus]] - Fellow soldier of Paul in the [[resources/people/pauline team/Philemon\|Philemon]] household, charged to complete his *diakonia* in Colossae.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Aristarchus\|Aristarchus]] - Thessalonian companion who endured the Ephesian riot and sailed with Paul as a prisoner to Rome.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Artemas\|Artemas]] - Coworker Paul considered sending to Crete to relieve [[resources/people/pauline team/Titus\|Titus]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Barnabas\|Barnabas]] - Cypriot Levite who sponsored Paul, co-led the first equipping servant journey, and invested in [[resources/people/pauline team/John Mark\|John Mark]] after Paul rejected him.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Carpus\|Carpus]] - Believer in Troas who held Paul's cloak, books, and parchments.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Claudia\|Claudia]] - Roman believer with an aristocratic name who sent greetings to [[resources/people/pauline team/Timothy\|Timothy]] during Paul's final imprisonment.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Clement\|Clement]] - Philippian coworker who labored with Paul in the gospel; possibly the later Clement of Rome.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Crescens\|Crescens]] - Coworker sent to Galatia during Paul's final imprisonment.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Demas\|Demas]] - Fellow worker who deserted Paul "because he loved this present world" and went to Thessalonica.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Epaphroditus\|Epaphroditus]] - Philippian envoy who carried their financial gift to Paul in Rome and nearly died in the process.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Epaphras\|Epaphras]] - Equipping servant who brought the gospel to Colossae, Laodicea, and Hierapolis, known for agonizing prayer.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Erastus\|Erastus]] - City treasurer of Corinth, the highest-ranking civic official in the Pauline network.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Eubulus\|Eubulus]] - Roman believer who sent greetings to [[resources/people/pauline team/Timothy\|Timothy]] during Paul's final imprisonment.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Euodia\|Euodia]] - Philippian woman who labored side by side with Paul in the gospel; in conflict with [[resources/people/pauline team/Syntyche\|Syntyche]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Fortunatus\|Fortunatus]] - Corinthian delegate who visited Paul in Ephesus with [[resources/people/pauline team/Stephanas\|Stephanas]] and [[resources/people/pauline team/Achaicus\|Achaicus]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Gaius\|Gaius]] - Corinthian believer Paul baptized, host to Paul and "the whole church" in Corinth.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Hermogenes\|Hermogenes]] - Asian believer who turned away from Paul during his final imprisonment.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Hymenaeus\|Hymenaeus]] - False teacher in Ephesus who taught the resurrection had already happened; handed over to Satan by Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Jason\|Jason]] - Thessalonian host who was dragged before city officials and forced to post bond for harboring Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Jesus Justus\|Jesus Justus]] - One of only three Jewish coworkers with Paul in Rome, a comfort to him during house arrest.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/John Mark\|John Mark]] - Failed helper on the first journey who was later restored, authored the Gospel of Mark.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Judas Barsabbas\|Judas Barsabbas]] - Jerusalem prophet who co-delivered the [[resources/concepts/Jerusalem Council\|Jerusalem Council]] decree to [[resources/concepts/Antioch Church\|Antioch]] with [[resources/people/pauline team/Silas\|Silas]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Junia\|Junia]] - Jewish believer in Rome, fellow prisoner of Paul, outstanding among the apostles, in Christ before Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Linus\|Linus]] - Roman believer identified by early church tradition as the first bishop of Rome after Peter and Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Lucius\|Lucius]] - Jewish believer from Cyrene, prophet and teacher in the [[resources/concepts/Antioch Church\|Antioch Church]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Luke\|Luke]] - Physician, Paul's travel companion, author of the Gospel of Luke and Acts.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Lydia\|Lydia]] - Seller of purple goods from Thyatira, first European convert, hosted the [[Philippian Church\|Philippian Church]] in her home.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Manaen\|Manaen]] - Prophet and teacher in [[resources/concepts/Antioch Church\|Antioch]], raised alongside Herod Antipas.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Mary (Rome)\|Mary (Rome)]] - Roman believer commended by Paul for having "worked very hard" for the congregation.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Mnason\|Mnason]] - Cypriot "early disciple" from the founding generation who hosted Paul in Jerusalem.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Nympha\|Nympha]] - Woman who hosted a house church in Laodicea in the Lycus Valley network.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Onesimus\|Onesimus]] - Runaway slave of [[resources/people/pauline team/Philemon\|Philemon]] who converted through Paul in prison; sent back with Paul's letter.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Onesiphorus\|Onesiphorus]] - Ephesian believer who searched for Paul in Rome and refreshed him, unashamed of his chains.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Persis\|Persis]] - Roman believer called "the beloved" by Paul, commended for having worked hard in the Lord.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Philemon\|Philemon]] - Wealthy Colossian believer, host of a house church, owner of [[resources/people/pauline team/Onesimus\|Onesimus]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Philetus\|Philetus]] - False teacher in Ephesus who partnered with [[resources/people/pauline team/Hymenaeus\|Hymenaeus]] in denying the future bodily resurrection.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Philip the evangelist\|Philip the evangelist]] - One of the Seven, preached in Samaria and to the Ethiopian eunuch, settled in Caesarea.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Phoebe\|Phoebe]] - Deacon and patron of the church at Cenchreae, bearer of Paul's letter to the Romans.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Phygelus\|Phygelus]] - Asian believer who turned away from Paul during his final imprisonment.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Priscilla\|Priscilla]] - Tentmaker, teacher of [[resources/people/pauline team/Apollos\|Apollos]], host of house churches in Ephesus and Rome, named before [[resources/people/pauline team/Aquila\|Aquila]] in four of six NT references.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Publius\|Publius]] - Chief official of Malta who hosted Paul's shipwrecked party; his father was healed by Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Pudens\|Pudens]] - Roman believer who sent greetings to [[resources/people/pauline team/Timothy\|Timothy]] during Paul's final imprisonment.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Quartus\|Quartus]] - Corinthian believer called "the brother," greeted alongside [[resources/people/pauline team/Erastus\|Erastus]] the city treasurer.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Secundus\|Secundus]] - Thessalonian delegate who carried the collection to Jerusalem with [[resources/people/pauline team/Aristarchus\|Aristarchus]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Silas\|Silas]] (also called Silvanus) - Jerusalem prophet and Roman citizen who partnered with Paul on the second journey; co-sender of both Thessalonian letters.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Simeon who was called Niger\|Simeon who was called Niger]] - Prophet and teacher of African descent in the [[resources/concepts/Antioch Church\|Antioch Church]] leadership team.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Sopater\|Sopater]] - Berean delegate in the collection journey to Jerusalem; probably the same as [[resources/people/pauline team/Sosipater\|Sosipater]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Sosipater\|Sosipater]] - Jewish believer from Berea present with Paul in Corinth when Romans was written.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Sosthenes\|Sosthenes]] - Corinthian synagogue ruler beaten before Gallio; co-sender of 1 Corinthians with Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Stephanas\|Stephanas]] - First convert in Achaia whose household devoted themselves to serving the saints in Corinth.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Syntyche\|Syntyche]] - Philippian woman who labored side by side with Paul in the gospel; in conflict with [[resources/people/pauline team/Euodia\|Euodia]].
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Tertius\|Tertius]] - Paul's scribe for Romans, the only amanuensis who inserts his own personal greeting into a Pauline letter.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Timothy\|Timothy]] - Paul's closest protege, co-sender of six letters, stationed in Ephesus, recipient of two pastoral epistles.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Titus\|Titus]] - Greek convert who served as Paul's envoy to Corinth and organizer of churches in Crete.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Trophimus\|Trophimus]] - Ephesian Gentile whose presence in Jerusalem triggered Paul's arrest at the temple.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Tryphena\|Tryphena]] - Roman believer commended for working hard in the Lord; paired with [[resources/people/pauline team/Tryphosa\|Tryphosa]], likely sisters.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Tryphosa\|Tryphosa]] - Roman believer commended for working hard in the Lord; paired with [[resources/people/pauline team/Tryphena\|Tryphena]], likely sisters.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Tychicus\|Tychicus]] - Asian coworker who carried Paul's letters to Colossae and Ephesus and reported on Paul's circumstances.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Urbanus\|Urbanus]] - Roman believer called "our fellow worker in Christ" by Paul.
- [[resources/people/pauline team/Zenas\|Zenas]] - Lawyer (Jewish or Roman) who traveled with [[resources/people/pauline team/Apollos\|Apollos]] through Crete.
