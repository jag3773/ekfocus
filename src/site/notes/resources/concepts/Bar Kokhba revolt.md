Jewish uprising against Rome (132-136 CE) led by Simon bar Kokhba, whom Rabbi Akiva endorsed as the Messiah. After initial success recapturing [[resources/concepts/Jerusalem\|Jerusalem]], Rome crushed the revolt, killed an estimated 580,000 Jews, renamed the province Syria Palaestina, and banned Jews from [[resources/concepts/Jerusalem\|Jerusalem]]. The revolt marked the end of Jewish sovereignty in the land until the modern era.
