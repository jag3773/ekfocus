## The Table of Nations and the Commission

Paul was aware that his commission was a reversal of the falling away at the tower of Babel [[resources/bible/pentateuch/Genesis 11\|Genesis 11]] of the nations that are listed in [[resources/bible/pentateuch/Genesis 10\|Genesis 10]]. In [[resources/bible/epistles/Romans 15#15:24\|Romans 15#15:24]], Paul tells the Romans that he still needs to get to "Spain," which is the same location as [[resources/concepts/Tarshish\|Tarshish]] in the Old Testament, found both in [[resources/bible/pentateuch/Genesis 10\|Genesis 10]] and [[resources/bible/prophets/Isaiah 66\|Isaiah 66]]. Now it's clear that Paul has the Isaiah passage in mind because he quotes from it in [[resources/bible/epistles/Romans 15#15 22\|Romans 15#15 22]]. At Pentecost, we only see [[Rome\|Rome]] mentioned as the farthest Western point so Paul is looking at the OT and determining that the gospel still needs preached in [[resources/concepts/Tarshish\|Tarshish]] (Spain) in order to complete the reversal of Babel.
