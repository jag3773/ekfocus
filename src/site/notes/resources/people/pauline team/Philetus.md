## Summary

Philetus appears once in [[resources/bible/epistles/2 Timothy 2#2:17\|2 Timothy 2#2:17]], paired with [[resources/people/pauline team/Hymenaeus\|Hymenaeus]] as a false teacher whose doctrine "will spread like gangrene." Their error: "saying that the resurrection has already happened, and they are upsetting the faith of some" ([[resources/bible/epistles/2 Timothy 2#2:18\|2 Timothy 2#2:18]]).

His name, Philetus ("beloved"), was common in the Greco-Roman world. He is otherwise unknown, no biblical or early church source mentions him. He served as Hymenaeus's partner in promoting over-realized eschatology that denied future bodily resurrection.

See [[resources/people/pauline team/Hymenaeus\|Hymenaeus]] for the full discussion of this theological error and its impact on the Ephesian church.
