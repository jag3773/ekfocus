# Reading ≠ Comprehension ≠ Recall ≠ Retention

These are separate stages:

- **Reading**: Exposure to concepts, ideas, stories.
- **Comprehension**: Grasping meaning.
- **Recall**: Retrieving information on demand.
- **Retention**: Maintaining knowledge over time.

Reading alone does not guarantee understanding. Comprehension does not ensure recall. Recall will not become retention if it's not committed to long term memory.

## Long-term Retention

Direct recall is challenging long-term. Stronger retention emerges through [[connected ideas\|connected ideas]] or [[spaced repetition\|spaced repetition]]. Nevertheless, if you make it through the first three steps you will still benefit from fuzzy knowledge about the domain and likely will remember where to find details, rather than verbatim quotes.
