# Tension - Allen Says Organization Suppresses Expansion While Johnson Says No Movement Survives Without It

The vault holds two convictions about organization and movement that pull hard against each other. Roland Allen, in [[allenSpontaneousExpansionChurch2018|The Spontaneous Expansion of the Church]], treats organized mission as the thing that chokes growth. He warns that establishing missions rather than churches breeds sterility and antagonism, and his whole project is to clear away the apparatus so a healthy church can expand of its own accord, a case developed in [[Spontaneous Expansion Is the Natural Activity of Healthy Christian Life|spontaneous expansion as the natural activity of healthy Christian life]]. For Allen the form itself can carry the harm, which is why he insists on [[Planting Churches Not Missions From the First Seed|planting churches, not missions, from the first seed]].

The vault's note [[Even Spontaneous Movements Must Find Institutional Form to Survive|that even spontaneous movements must find institutional form to survive]] argues the reverse. Reading John Johnson, it states flatly that the most spontaneous and prophetic of movements cannot last unless they find institutional form, and it reads the New Testament as already moving that way, with defined office from the start of Acts and Paul founding a learning institute at Ephesus. On this account structure is what lets a movement endure past its first generation.

The collision is real because each side locates the threat to a movement in the opposite place. Allen sees organization as the disease and spontaneity as health. Johnson sees unstructured spontaneity as the thing that burns out and institutional form as the cure. Set the strongest version of each together and they cannot both be simply true: either the apparatus suppresses the life, or the absence of form lets the life dissipate.

A resolution may run through the question Johnson himself poses, which form serves multiplication rather than smothering it. Allen never opposed a church having elders and order. He opposed a foreign institution standing in for the native church, the displacement traced in [[The Mission Station Substitutes a Foreign Institution for the Native Church|the mission station that substitutes a foreign institution for the native church]]. Johnson might grant that indigenous, reproducible structure is precisely what he means, while warning Allen that even indigenous movements ossify or evaporate without deliberate form. The unresolved edge is whether the structure a movement needs to survive is the same structure Allen blamed for killing it, only renamed. The vault keeps a near neighbor in [[Patient Ferment Complicates the Programmatic Mission Instinct|the patient ferment that complicates the programmatic mission instinct]], where the dispute is likewise about whether managing a movement protects it or kills it.
