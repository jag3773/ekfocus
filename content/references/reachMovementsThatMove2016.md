## Abstract
7 Root Principles That Nurture Church Planting Movements  Discover the keys to exponential growth!  Churches around the world are seeing more than church growth. They are seeing exponential church multiplication, tens of thousands of baptisms and thousands of new churches. After years of extensive statistical research along with personal experience in facilitating a church planting movement, Robert M. Reach has uncovered the Seven Root Principles that produce movement growth. Based on these root principles, Movements that Move provides concrete instruction on how to implement the principles, how to start a movement and how to cultivate one after it gets going. Endorsements "Robert knows movements. Now he has gathered his knowledge and experience in a book that is both comprehensive and clear." - from the Foreword by Steve Addison Director of MOVE and author of Pioneering Movements "Robert has penned a helpful book, which explores movement growth in non-Western contexts. While his insights will need translation to be adopted by Western churches, books involving movement thinking are rare and valuable in this critical time. Thank you Robert." -Alan Hirsch Missional Strategist and Author of The Forgotten Ways
