## Abstract
Come And Go is about living on mission with God. Every believer is called to participate in God's mission, one of the greatest privileges as a Christ follower. God has invited us to both come to Him- living in intimate relationship with our Creator and go for Him- sharing the life-transforming gospel with others. You will be inspired by the personal stories and scriptural examples of those who overcame the challenges and embarked on the exciting adventure of living on mission with God by the power of the Holy Spirit. This book will help you discover simple practical ways you too can answer God's call. None of us are truly qualified, but it is God and His life in us that qualifies us.
