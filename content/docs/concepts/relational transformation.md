People change through relationship, not force. This concept threads through theology, organizational theory, and counseling in the vault.

## The Pattern

The [[The Roles of the Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit empowers and fills believers]] for tasks they cannot accomplish alone. God's three instruments for transformation are the Word, the Spirit, and the People of God ([[2026-04-13 Synthesis - Pflaeging and Crabb|Crabb]]). Pflaeging's Three Rs (Relate, Repeat, Reframe) describe the same shape in organizations: change starts with a relationship to someone who embodies the desired future ([[2026-04-13 Synthesis - Pflaeging and Crabb|Pflaeging and Crabb synthesis]]).

[[The Power of Vulnerability|Brené Brown]] names the posture this requires: uncertainty, risk, emotional exposure. [[2008 Manhood-Leads Courageously|Godly manhood]] calls it leading courageously, rejecting passivity and accepting responsibility. [[2024-09-27 Beale Revelation Highlights|Beale's reading of Revelation]] shows the oldest version of the pattern: suffering for Christ seals victory over the powers of darkness, because the cross itself transformed tragedy into triumph.

## Where It Shows Up

Pflaeging's Beta organizations scatter authority to the people closest to the work. Crabb's Relational Model says people were made for relationship and impact, and their dysfunction traces to pursuing those longings through independence. Brown's research confirms that groups built on vulnerability outperform those built on control. The [[2026-03 ETEN Post Steering Committee Baleigh|ETEN leadership restructuring in 2026]] distributes operational authority along these lines.
