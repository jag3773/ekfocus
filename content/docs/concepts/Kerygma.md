- Dodd's [[doddApostolicPreachingIts1937|The apostolic preaching and its developments]] covers what he thinks the kerygma is in detail, especially early in the book. Not so much the act of preaching but the message. Not so much teaching or exhortation but gospel proclamation.
- Thiselton's [[thiseltonHermeneuticsIntroduction2009|Hermeneutics]] Notes that Bultman and Fuchs both make a big deal of this.  See subject index therein for *kerygma*. Bultman is contrasting with "banal teaching."
- Baird summarizes in [[bairdWhatKerygmaStudy1957]] that the *kerygma* proclaims:
  - the age of fulfillment (*now* and not yet)
  - came about through JC life, death, resurrection
  - Jesus  (vindicated by resurrection and exalted to right hand of God
  - the HS in the church is manifestation of Christ's power and glory
  - the Messianic age of fulfillment will be fully consummated when christ returns (now and *not yet*)
  - an appeal for repentance, the promise of forgiveness and salvation (and invitation to new life and participation in the family of God)
  - See [[1 Corinthians 15]] verses 3-8 and [[Galatians 1]] verses 11-17
- See also Paul's recounting of his commission in [[Acts 26]], especially verses 16-23
- [[mcdonaldKerygmaDidacheArticulation1980]] is opposed to a strong distinction between *kerygma and didache*
- Reed talks about this in [[reedBILDEncyclicals2017#Page 15]], he says there are 3 things:
  - "time is fulfilled"
  - Kingdom of God is near
  - new of the victory is being proclaimed
  - See [[Ephesians 2]]
- See [[1 Corinthians 15]]:1-6
