## The Table of Nations and the Commission

Paul was aware that his commission was a reversal of the falling away at the tower of Babel [[Genesis 11]] of the nations that are listed in [[Genesis 10]]. In [[Romans 15#15:24]], Paul tells the Romans that he still needs to get to "Spain," which is the same location as [[Tarshish]] in the Old Testament, found both in [[Genesis 10]] and [[Isaiah 66]]. Now it's clear that Paul has the Isaiah passage in mind because he quotes from it in [[Romans 15#15 22]]. At Pentecost, we only see [[Rome]] mentioned as the farthest Western point so Paul is looking at the OT and determining that the gospel still needs preached in [[Tarshish]] (Spain) in order to complete the reversal of Babel.
