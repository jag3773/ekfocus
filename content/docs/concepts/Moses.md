The prophet and lawgiver through whom God delivered Israel from Egypt and gave the [[Mosaic Covenant]]. God called him from the burning bush at [[Horeb]] ([[Exodus 3]]), led Israel through the sea ([[Exodus 14]]), and gave the law at Sinai ([[Exodus 20]]). Scripture calls him the one with whom God spoke "face to face" ([[Numbers 12#12:6-8|Numbers 12:6-8]]) and promises a prophet like him ([[Deuteronomy 18#18:15-18|Deuteronomy 18:15-18]]). He died on Mount Nebo in sight of the land he did not enter ([[Deuteronomy 34]]). Hebrews contrasts him as a faithful servant in God's house with Christ as the Son over it ([[Hebrews 3#3:1-6|Hebrews 3:1-6]]).

Tradition names Moses as the author of the [[Torah]] (the [[Pentateuch]]); how far that holds is taken up in [[Biblical Authorship]].
