The first five books of the Bible ([[Genesis 1|Genesis]], [[Exodus 19|Exodus]], [[Leviticus 23|Leviticus]], [[Numbers 1|Numbers]], [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy]]), also called the [[Pentateuch]]. Torah means "instruction" or "teaching" in Hebrew. Given to Israel through [[Moses]] as part of the [[Mosaic Covenant]], it contains narrative, law, and liturgical instruction.
