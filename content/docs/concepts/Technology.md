Technology is the making and use of tools, techniques and systems to extend human reach. The vault reads it two ways at once. Theologically, making things is part of the creation mandate, and the biblical story moves from a garden to a city. Critically, no tool is neutral: each medium reshapes the people who use it and the ecology it enters, and it can extend ministry without replacing embodied disciple-making. This is the hub; specific threads live in the notes and MOCs below.
